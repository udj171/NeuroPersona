    Understanding Your Scores

    Corrected Scores (1-5 Scale)

    Your raw responses have been processed through our advanced 7-step algorithm to produce corrected scores on a 1-5 scale, with 3 representing average and 1-2 below average, 4-5 above average.

    1 = Very Low 2 = Low 3 = Average 4 = High 5 = Very High

    Confidence Intervals

    Each score includes a 95% confidence interval, showing the range where your true score likely falls. Wider intervals indicate less certainty; narrower ones indicate higher confidence in the measurement.

    Population Percentiles

    Percentiles show what percentage of the population scored lower than you on each domain. A 75th percentile means you scored higher than 75% of the population on that trait.

    Deception Correction

    Our algorithms detect response patterns indicating social desirability bias or deliberate deception and apply mathematical corrections to produce more authentic scores.
